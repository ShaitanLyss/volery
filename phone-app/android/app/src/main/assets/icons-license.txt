Icons used by the Volery phone app
==================================

Only two glyphs in app/src/main/kotlin/com/volery/phone/Glyph.kt are not drawn
in this codebase:

- Glyph.Bird is Lucide's `bird` (lucide-static v1.71.0, https://lucide.dev),
  translated from its 24-unit viewBox into the app's 16-unit one. It is the
  perched bird, shown while the ears are closed.
- Glyph.BirdFlying is Font Awesome Free's `dove` (6.7.2,
  https://fontawesome.com), scaled from its 512-unit viewBox into the app's 16
  and drawn as the path's own solid shape. It is the open-winged bird, shown
  while the ears are open.
